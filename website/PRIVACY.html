What we collect

  • Account data: name, email, phone, city, country, gotra, timezone — provided by you when you sign up or edit your profile.
  • Priest data: name, title, temple affiliation, specializations, languages, experience, and photos of pujas performed — provided by priests when they register or update their portfolio.
  • Booking data: puja selected, date/time, priest assigned, sankalp name, and price — created when you make a booking.
  • Payment data: payments are processed by Stripe; Sanskar does not receive or store your card number, CVC, or full payment credentials. We store only the booking amount and payment status (paid/pending).
  • Photos: priests may upload photos of pujas they performed; these are visible to devotees browsing that priest's profile.

What we do NOT collect

  • Passwords are never stored in plain text — only a salted, one-way cryptographic hash (PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256).
  • We do not access your contacts, camera roll (beyond a photo you explicitly pick), location, or SMS.
